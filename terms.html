This website is operated by EG TECH to showcase digital technology capabilities, engineering case studies, service offerings, and interactive briefing tools for founders and growing businesses.

You agree to browse and use this website only for legitimate informational and enquiry purposes. You must not attempt to compromise website security, submit malicious code, or scrape proprietary copy and brand assets without prior written consent.

General Website vs. Client Engagements: Browsing this website or preparing a preliminary project brief does not constitute a binding contract to deliver services. Client engagements are governed solely by separate written proposals and agreements signed or confirmed before kickoff.

2. Service Enquiries and Proposals

When you prepare a project brief using our client-side briefing tool, your input is processed privately in your browser without automatic network transmission. When you choose to send your brief via email or discuss requirements, EG TECH reviews your goals to prepare a structured scope or proposal.

Initial consultations, discussions, and estimates do not commit either party until formal deliverables, responsibilities, review milestones, and commercial terms are mutually documented in writing.
